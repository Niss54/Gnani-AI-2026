# MSMED Act 2006 — Section 15 & 16 Statutory Recovery Guidelines

## 1. Statutory Payment Window (Section 15)
Under Section 15 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006, where any buyer purchases goods or receives services from any registered micro or small enterprise (MSME with valid Udyam Registration Number):
- The buyer shall make payment therefor on or before the agreed date.
- In no case shall the agreed period between buyer and supplier exceed **forty-five (45) days** from the day of acceptance or the day of deemed acceptance.
- If there is no agreed written period, payment must be made within **fifteen (15) days**.

## 2. Mandatory Compound Penal Interest (Section 16)
Where any buyer fails to make payment of the amount to the supplier as required under Section 15, the buyer is statutorily liable to pay compound interest with monthly rests to the supplier on that amount from the appointed day:
- **Rate of Penal Interest:** The penal interest rate is fixed at **three times (3x) the Bank Rate notified by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**.
- **Compounding Frequency:** The interest compounds with **monthly rests**.
- **Non-Waivable Right:** Section 16 is an absolute statutory right. Any contract clause claiming zero interest or waiver of late fees is legally void under Section 24 (Overriding Effect of MSMED Act).

## 3. Disallowance of Tax Deduction (Section 23)
Notwithstanding anything contained in the Income Tax Act, 1961, the amount of any interest payable or paid by any buyer, under or in accordance with the provisions of this Act, **shall not be allowed as deduction** in computing the taxable income of such buyer.

## 4. MSEFC Arbitration & Fast-Track Decree (Section 18)
Any party to a dispute with respect to any amount due under Section 17 may make a reference to the **Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council (MSEFC)**:
- Conciliation proceedings are mandatory within 90 days.
- If conciliation fails, statutory arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 is triggered.
- Under Section 19, an enterprise buyer cannot appeal or challenge an MSEFC award in court without pre-depositing **75% of the decree amount** with the court.
