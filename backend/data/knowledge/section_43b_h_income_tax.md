# Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act — MSME Overdue Deduction Disallowance

## 1. Statutory Background & Mandate
Introduced by the Finance Act 2023 with effect from Assessment Year 2024-25, Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 mandates that any sum payable by the assessee (buyer) to a registered micro or small enterprise beyond the time limit specified in Section 15 of the MSMED Act, 2006 shall be allowed as a deduction **only in the previous year in which such sum is actually paid**.

## 2. Practical Business Impact on Enterprise Buyers
If a buyer receives deliverables from an MSME supplier and fails to clear the outstanding invoice within 45 days (or 15 days without agreement):
- The entire overdue invoice amount is **added back to the buyer's net taxable income** for that fiscal year.
- At an effective corporate tax rate of 25% to 35% plus surcharge and cess, delayed payments result in severe immediate tax cash-outflows for the defaulting debtor.
- Even if the debtor accounts for the expenditure on an accrual basis, the deduction is strictly denied until actual proof of bank remittance / settlement is produced.
